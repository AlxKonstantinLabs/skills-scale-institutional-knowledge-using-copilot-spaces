# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA / Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA / Testing Leads ensure the product meets quality standards before release. They define testing strategy, validate acceptance criteria, and help the team reduce defects and rework.

### Responsibilities
- Define test plans and release readiness criteria
- Coordinate automated and manual testing across features and integrations
- Capture, triage, and follow up on defects with engineering owners
- Validate that delivered work meets acceptance criteria and business intent
- Support release readiness decisions with evidence from test results

### Goals
- Catch defects early and reduce production risk
- Improve test coverage and validation quality
- Enable predictable release decisions and customer confidence

### Typical Communication
- Test plans and defect triage sessions
- Release readiness reviews and QA sign-off checkpoints
- Feedback loops with Developers, PMs, and Product Leads on acceptance gaps

### How this role interacts with existing roles
- Works closely with Developers to reproduce issues, validate fixes, and improve quality practices.
- Partners with Product Managers and Project Managers to confirm that features meet requirements and release timing remains realistic.
- Provides objective validation that helps Product Leads and stakeholders make evidence-based decisions.

---

## Tech Lead / Engineering Lead

### Role Summary
Tech Leads guide engineering execution, technical direction, and design quality. They balance delivery speed with technical sustainability and help teams make sound architecture decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical direction, standards, and architectural guardrails
- Review technical design choices and implementation trade-offs
- Mentor developers and support complex technical problem solving
- Identify and escalate technical risks, dependencies, and delivery blockers
- Help maintain code quality, maintainability, and operational readiness

### Goals
- Maintain technical excellence while enabling delivery
- Reduce architectural drift and rework
- Unblock teams on difficult engineering decisions

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Engineering planning and dependency reviews
- Escalation of high-risk technical issues to PMs and stakeholders

### How this role interacts with existing roles
- Collaborates with Developers to align implementation choices and support coding standards.
- Works with Product Managers to translate business goals into feasible technical scope and trade-offs.
- Supports Project Managers by identifying delivery risks, dependencies, and realistic sequencing.

---

## Security Lead

### Role Summary
Security Leads protect the project from avoidable risk by embedding security practices into planning, coding, testing, and release activities.

### Responsibilities
- Review security requirements and project risk areas
- Conduct threat assessments and security reviews for new features or changes
- Work with engineering to validate secure design patterns and remediation steps
- Coordinate security scanning, compliance checks, and incident response support
- Communicate risk levels and required mitigations to project leadership

### Goals
- Reduce vulnerabilities and protect customer trust
- Ensure security is built into delivery rather than treated as a late-stage gate
- Maintain compliance with relevant standards and policies

### Typical Communication
- Security reviews, threat modeling, and remediation discussions
- Risk updates during planning, release reviews, and incident response
- Clear escalation paths for high-risk findings and urgent security issues

### How this role interacts with existing roles
- Partners with Developers and Tech Leads to ensure secure implementation choices and timely remediation.
- Aligns with Product Managers and Project Managers on risk, priority, and release readiness when security findings affect scope or timing.
- Supports Stakeholders and other decision-makers with risk-based guidance for trade-offs and escalations.

---

## DevOps / Infrastructure Engineer

### Role Summary
DevOps / Infrastructure Engineers support reliable delivery by managing environments, automation, deployment pipelines, and operational health.

### Responsibilities
- Design and maintain CI/CD pipelines and deployment workflows
- Manage infrastructure, environments, configuration, and observability tools
- Support release execution, rollback planning, and production readiness checks
- Monitor service health and help triage operational incidents
- Enable the team to deploy safely and repeatedly with minimal manual overhead

### Goals
- Improve deployment reliability and platform stability
- Reduce operational friction and release risk
- Maintain a healthy, observable production environment

### Typical Communication
- Deployment readiness reviews and release coordination
- Incidents, production health updates, and infrastructure risk discussions
- Collaboration with engineering teams on environment and automation needs

### How this role interacts with existing roles
- Works with Developers and Tech Leads to ensure the application can be deployed and supported reliably.
- Coordinates with Project Managers on release windows, rollback plans, and operational dependencies.
- Provides critical support to Product Leaders and Stakeholders by helping teams deliver value without destabilizing core systems.

---

## UX / Design Lead

### Role Summary
UX / Design Leads shape the user experience and ensure the product remains intuitive, usable, and aligned with customer needs.

### Responsibilities
- Define user flows, interaction models, and design standards
- Conduct usability research and turn insights into actionable design decisions
- Review product experience against customer goals and accessibility expectations
- Collaborate with Product Managers and engineering to refine features and reduce friction
- Validate that implementation matches the intended UX and business intent

### Goals
- Deliver clear, valuable, and accessible product experiences
- Reduce confusion, rework, and support burden
- Improve adoption and satisfaction through thoughtful design

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews, user research findings, and prototype walkthroughs
- Collaboration with Product Managers on problem framing and prioritization
- Feedback loops with Developers and QA on implementation fidelity and usability issues

### How this role interacts with existing roles
- Aligns with Product Managers to ensure customer value and experience are represented in planning decisions.
- Partner with Developers to refine features and ensure implementation matches the intended user journey.
- Works with QA to validate that the final experience meets usability and accessibility expectations before release.

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters and Agile Coaches help the team work effectively by improving process, removing impediments, and enabling sustainable delivery practices.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, standups, retrospectives, and team rituals
- Identify blockers, dependencies, and process issues that slow delivery
- Coach the team on agile practices and continuous improvement habits
- Support healthy collaboration across cross-functional roles
- Help the team maintain focus on value delivery and clear commitments

### Goals
- Improve team flow and delivery consistency
- Reduce friction caused by process confusion or unresolved blockers
- Build a healthier, more collaborative working rhythm

### Typical Communication
- Retrospectives, team rituals, and process improvement discussions
- Dependency tracking and blocker escalation support
- Coaching conversations with Project Managers, Product Leads, and engineering teams

### How this role interacts with existing roles
- Works with Project Managers to improve execution rhythm without adding unnecessary process overhead.
- Supports Product Managers and Developers by clarifying priorities, improving handoffs, and reducing team friction.
- Helps stakeholders understand delivery realities while keeping the team aligned on commitments and incremental progress.

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
Business Analysts bridge business needs and implementation by clarifying requirements, validating value, and helping teams turn goals into actionable work.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and document business requirements and process context
- Translate needs into user stories, acceptance criteria, and defined outcomes
- Validate that proposed solutions address the root problem and business priorities
- Help align cross-functional teams around scope, constraints, and trade-offs
- Support stakeholder communication and change impact understanding

### Goals
- Reduce ambiguity in scope and requirements
- Improve alignment between business intent and delivery execution
- Increase confidence that work creates measurable value

### Typical Communication
- Requirements workshops and stakeholder interviews
- Acceptance criteria validation and backlog refinement sessions
- Updates on scope, dependencies, and business impact to project and product teams

### How this role interacts with existing roles
- Partners with Product Managers to refine problem statements and outcomes.
- Works with Developers and QA to ensure implementation and validation reflect the intended business meaning.
- Helps Project Managers identify dependencies, risks, and sequencing concerns tied to business priorities.

---

## Stakeholder / Sponsor Representative

### Role Summary
Stakeholder or Sponsor Representatives provide business direction, resource support, and decision-making authority for the project. They help connect project work to strategic goals and organizational priorities.

### Responsibilities
- Define business context, expected outcomes, and strategic priorities
- Approve scope trade-offs, milestones, and key decisions when needed
- Remove blockers that require executive support or cross-functional alignment
- Provide feedback on progress, business value, and strategic fit
- Champion the initiative across teams and leadership audiences

### Goals
- Ensure work remains aligned to business objectives and usable ROI
- Support teams with clear decision-making and adequate sponsorship
- Drive organizational momentum for successful adoption and benefit realization

### Typical Communication
- Status reviews, milestone checks, and steering group discussions
- Escalation of high-level decisions, risks, or business constraints
- Briefings on expected value, outcomes, and major project decisions

### How this role interacts with existing roles
- Works with Product Managers and Project Managers to set outcomes, prioritize trade-offs, and support delivery decisions.
- Collaborates with Developers, QA, and Tech Leads through risk and milestone reviews that influence scope or release timing.
- Helps Product Leads and project teams prioritize work that best supports business value and stakeholder confidence.

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- These roles work together as a delivery system: Product and Project leadership define direction, engineering and QA turn that direction into reality, and stakeholders provide sponsorship and strategic alignment.
